The actual mechanism

Everything an agent can do, split down the middle

Read and compute freely — that's where the reasoning happens. The instant an action would change what's true in the platform, it stops being code and becomes paperwork.

READ · COMPUTE

What agents do unsupervised

  • →Pull live stock, order, and staffing data
  • →Run the weighted-moving-average forecast
  • →Score a dynamic-pricing signal from active order volume
  • →Search the ops manual for HACCP or labor-law answers
  • →Draft a purchase order, campaign, or roster — as text
PROPOSE → HUMAN → EXECUTE

What still requires a click

  • ✓Sending that purchase order to a supplier
  • ✓Changing a menu price — PATCH /api/menu is on no agent's allowlist, ever
  • ✓Publishing a shift roster
  • ✓Taking a churn campaign live
  • ✓Processing a customer refund
The seven, plus the one they answer to

Specialists, not one god-agent

Each one narrow enough to reason about and audit on its own. The Manager Copilot doesn't replace them — it's the one thing a manager actually has to talk to.

01

Demand Forecast

Weighted moving average, cron at 2am — numbers a manager can trace back to real order history.

02

Inventory Reorder

Watches stock every six hours; drafts a PO the moment something runs low.

03

Dynamic Pricing

Reads live order load — even weather, via Open-Meteo — and suggests, never patches.

04

Churn Prevention

Segments at-risk customers daily, drafts one retention campaign per run.

05

Review Response

A 1★ review lands on the bus; a draft reply lands in the queue minutes later.

06

Shift Optimisation

Weekly, cross-references staff availability against the forecast to draft a roster.

07

Kitchen Coach

Nightly prep-time metrics become one coaching brief per store, not a lecture.

— CONDUCTOR

Manager Copilot

The one thing a manager talks to. Fans out to all seven, answers from the ops manual, holds the approval queue.

Enforced, not suggested

What a manager's click actually does

Approve one proposal, and the ledger notices

Each resolved proposal is hashed together with the one before it. Edit a record after the fact — the record itself, not the draft — and every hash after it stops matching.
