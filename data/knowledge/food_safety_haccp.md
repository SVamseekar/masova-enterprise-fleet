# MaSoVa Operations Manual — Food Safety and HACCP

**Document ID:** MOS-FS-001  
**Owner:** Group Food Safety (Paris fleet)  
**Applies to:** All MaSoVa pizza restaurants in France  
**Audience:** Store managers, kitchen leads, opening/closing duty managers  
**Related:** MOS-EQ-001 (equipment), MOS-SU-001 (suppliers)

This is the controlling HACCP policy for the fleet. Live stock counts, ticket times, and cooler probe logs live in the store system of record — not in this document. Agents and the Manager Copilot may quote this policy; they must not invent temperatures, hold times, or “all-clear” verdicts that contradict it.

---

## 1. Purpose and legal frame

MaSoVa prepares pizza, salads, and sides for dine-in, collection, and delivery. High-risk foods include raw and cooked proteins, dairy, dough, cut produce, and ready-to-eat (RTE) garnishes.

Managers must run a documented HACCP plan consistent with Regulation (EC) No 852/2004, French hygiene rules for restaurants, and municipal inspection practice in Paris. Critical control points (CCPs) in this fleet are: receiving temperature, cold storage (cooler temperature), hot holding, cook / reheat core temperature, and allergen labelling at the pass.

A failed CCP is a **stop-and-fix** event, not a “note for later.” The kitchen lead records the time, product, probe reading, and disposition (use, move, discard, facilities ticket).

---

## 2. Cold chain — cooler temperature and freezers

Walk-in coolers and reach-in cold storage must hold product between **0 °C and 4 °C**. That cooler temperature band is the CCP for dairy, raw protein, prepared salads, and opened RTE.

- Probe at **open**, **mid-shift**, and **close**. Write the reading on the HACCP log with the staff initials.
- If cooler temperature stays **above 5 °C for more than 30 minutes**, move high-risk foods to a working unit, discard product that has been in the danger zone beyond the two-hour rule below, and open a facilities ticket. Do not “wait and see” through a lunch rush.
- Do not load a recovering cooler with a new chilled delivery until it is back in band.

Freezer cabinets: **−18 °C or colder**. Do not refreeze product that has fully thawed. Partial thaw in a retarder under a documented dough programme is allowed; once dough has been bench-proofed for service, leftover balls follow the 72-hour RTE rule, not a return to frozen.

---

## 3. Receiving

Accept chilled deliveries only when **product surface temperature** is within the cooler range (0–4 °C). Probe a representative carton, not only the truck air.

Reject and photograph:

- Warm dairy or protein
- Damaged, leaking, or unlabelled packaging
- Broken cold-chain seals
- Allergen-critical lines with missing specification sheets

Separate raw proteins from RTE on dedicated shelves (raw below RTE). Date-label every opened container. Maximum **72 hours** for prepared RTE after opening or production, unless a shorter supplier use-by applies.

---

## 4. Cooking, hot holding, and reheating

- Cook poultry and mixed pizza toppings to a **core of ≥ 75 °C**.
- Hot-hold cabinets keep food at **≥ 63 °C**. Discard product held below that for more than **two hours**. Do not mix a recovered pan into a fresh pan to “average” the temperature.
- Reheat once to **≥ 75 °C** core before service. A second reheat is not permitted; discard.

Deck ovens: if bake is uneven (pale centre, dark rim), follow MOS-EQ-001. Food-safety disposition still applies: underbaked pizza does not leave the pass.

---

## 5. Hand hygiene and cross-contamination

Staff wash hands after raw product, waste, money, or returning from the floor. Colour-coded boards:

| Colour | Use |
|--------|-----|
| Red | Raw meat |
| Blue | Fish |
| Green | Vegetables |
| Yellow | Poultry |
| White | Dairy / RTE |

Cloths: sanitiser buckets changed at least every two hours in service. Never wipe a raw board then a pass plate with the same cloth.

---

## 6. Allergens

MaSoVa standard allergen matrix (EU 1169/2011) must be current on the menu and POS. Substitutions that change gluten, dairy, nuts, egg, fish, soya, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites, lupin, or molluscs require manager confirmation and a ticket reprint. The copilot must not invent allergen advice; it points at this matrix and the live menu.

---

## 7. Two-hour / four-hour rule (danger zone)

Product in the 5–63 °C band:

- **Under 2 hours:** may be refrigerated and used, or cooked/served immediately.
- **2–4 hours:** use immediately; do not return to cold hold.
- **Over 4 hours:** discard. Record on the waste and HACCP logs.

---

## 8. What managers must not do

- Override a cooler alarm without a probe reading in the log.
- Ask an agent to “approve” a delivery that failed temperature.
- Continue service from a unit that has been out of band for more than 30 minutes without moving high-risk stock.

---

## 9. Escalation

Facilities: refrigeration, gas, or oven faults (see MOS-EQ-001).  
District manager: repeated CCP failures in a week, inspector visit, suspected foodborne illness.  
Do not use customer chat or public review replies to discuss an active incident.
