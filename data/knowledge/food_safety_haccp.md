# Food Safety — HACCP (Paris / EU restaurant ops)

Operational guidance for MaSoVa kitchen managers. Numbers here are regulatory
targets and SOP ranges — not live inventory counts.

## Cooler temperature controls

Walk-in coolers and reach-in cold storage must hold product between **0 °C and
4 °C** (32–39 °F). Check cooler temperature at open, mid-shift, and close.
Record readings on the HACCP log. If the cooler temperature drifts above 5 °C
for more than 30 minutes, move high-risk foods (dairy, raw protein, prepared
salads) to a working unit and call facilities.

Freezer cabinets: −18 °C or colder. Do not refreeze thawed product.

## Receiving and storage

Accept chilled deliveries only when product surface temperature is within the
cooler range. Separate raw proteins from ready-to-eat items on dedicated shelves.
Label opened containers with date and use-by (max 72 hours for prepared RTE).

## Hand hygiene and cross-contamination

Staff wash hands after handling raw product, waste, or money. Colour-coded boards:
red (raw meat), blue (fish), green (veg), yellow (poultry), white (dairy/RTE).

## Hot holding

Hot-hold cabinets keep food at ≥ 63 °C. Discard product held below that for more
than two hours. Reheat once to ≥ 75 °C core temperature before service.
