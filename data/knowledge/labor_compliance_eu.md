# MaSoVa Operations Manual — Labour and Scheduling (France)

**Document ID:** MOS-HR-001  
**Owner:** People Operations (France)  
**Applies to:** All MaSoVa restaurants in France  
**Audience:** Store managers, assistant managers, district managers  
**Related:** Shift Optimisation agent (drafts only); MOS-FS-001 (cannot be waived for coverage)

This policy states how MaSoVa schedules restaurant labour in France. Exact hours, contracts, and premiums live in the HR system and the posted rota. The Shift Optimisation agent may **propose** a roster; it never publishes a rota or forces overtime. Managers approve or reject in the console.

---

## 1. Working time

Weekly averages must respect the French Labour Code (Code du travail): legal weekly working time, maximum daily hours, and rest. Continuous service (lunch through late evening) still requires **paid breaks** as posted for the site.

- Do not schedule a team member above contracted hours without a recorded agreement and the applicable premium.
- Minors and apprentices: stricter evening cut-offs and prohibited tasks (fryer, late close, alcohol service where applicable). If the draft rota places a minor after cut-off, reject the proposal.

The copilot must not quote a personal hour total that did not come from the live roster tools.

---

## 2. Rest between shifts

A consecutive rest period is required between close and the next open. Split doubles that erase overnight rest need **People Operations approval**, not a verbal “we’re short.”

Sunday and public-holiday work follows the store’s collective agreement and the published calendar. The agent may flag understaffing; it may not waive rest.

---

## 3. Timekeeping, pay, and tips

Clock-in accuracy is a payroll and audit control. Managers correct missed punches in the time system, not in chat.

Tip pooling follows the **posted store policy** in the break room and the applicable agreement. Do not invent cash splits, off-book envelopes, or “the AI said to share evenly.” Customer-facing agents must never discuss another employee’s pay.

---

## 4. Sick leave, call-outs, and coverage

When a cook or cashier calls out:

1. Record the absence in the HR / rota tool.
2. Offer coverage from the trained bench in seniority / availability order.
3. If a draft overtime or swap is needed, the Shift agent (or the manager) **proposes**; the manager **approves**.
4. Do not force overtime beyond agreed caps. Escalate persistent gaps to the district manager.

Food-safety certification (MOS-FS-001) is not waived because the kitchen is short.

---

## 5. Equal treatment and scheduling fairness

Rotas must not systematically assign closing, late delivery, or unpaid extra minutes to the same people. Preference notes (studies, childcare) are honoured when they are in the HR file. The Shift agent uses forecast covers and skill tags only — not informal “who usually stays.”

---

## 6. What the Shift Optimisation agent may do

| Allowed | Not allowed |
|---------|-------------|
| Read forecast covers, skill mix, contracted hours | Publish the rota |
| Draft a roster that respects rest and caps | Approve its own draft |
| Flag underload / overload vs forecast | Invent names not on the store roster |

---

## 7. Escalation

People Operations: contract questions, disputes, inspection.  
District manager: inability to cover a service while staying inside this policy.  
Works council / CSE topics are out of scope for the copilot.
