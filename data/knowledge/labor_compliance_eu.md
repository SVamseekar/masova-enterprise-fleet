# Labor Compliance — EU / France restaurant ops

Guidance for Paris store managers. Prefer official HR calendars for exact
hours; this manual covers common checks only.

## Working time

Standard weekly averages must respect French labour code limits. Track breaks
during continuous service. Minors and apprentices have stricter evening cut-offs.

## Rest between shifts

Ensure consecutive rest periods between closing and next open. Do not schedule
split doubles that erase overnight rest without HR approval.

## Timekeeping and tips

Clock-in accuracy matters for payroll and audit. Tip pooling rules follow the
store policy posted in the break room — do not invent cash distributions in chat.

## Sick leave and replacement

When a cook calls out, managers draft a coverage proposal; they do not force
overtime beyond agreed caps. Escalate persistent gaps to the district manager.
