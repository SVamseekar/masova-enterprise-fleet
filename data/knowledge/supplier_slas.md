# Supplier SLAs (Paris fleet)

Contract expectations for dry, chilled, and produce suppliers serving MaSoVa
Paris stores. Live purchase quantities come from inventory tools — never from
this manual.

## Delivery windows

Chilled lines target morning windows so coolers restock before lunch. Late
deliveries beyond the SLA grace period are logged; managers draft a PO note,
not an automatic reorder qty.

## Fill rate and substitutions

Preferred fill rate is 95%+ of ordered lines. Substitutions require manager OK
when allergen or recipe impact is material. Record rejected lines with reason
codes for the weekly vendor review.

## Temperature on arrival

Chilled goods must arrive within HACCP cooler ranges. Reject warm dairy or
protein and photograph the probe reading for the claim file.

## Invoice disputes

Flag price variance vs. contracted rate within five business days. Do not
approve invoice payment from the agent chat — finance owns settlement.
