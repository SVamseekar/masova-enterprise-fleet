# MaSoVa Operations Manual — Kitchen Equipment

**Document ID:** MOS-EQ-001  
**Owner:** Facilities and Kitchen Operations  
**Applies to:** All MaSoVa Paris kitchens  
**Audience:** Store managers, kitchen leads  
**Related:** MOS-FS-001 (food disposition during equipment failure)

Managers follow this SOP for common faults. Gas, electrical, and sealed refrigeration work is **facilities only**. Do not invent meter readings, bypass cut-outs, or ask the copilot for a “workaround” that contradicts a safety interlock.

The Kitchen Coach agent may summarise ticket times and bottlenecks from live orders. It does not certify that an oven is safe to run.

---

## 1. Deck oven — uneven bake

**Symptoms:** pale centre, dark rim; one side blistered.

1. Confirm stone seating and that the deck is at set point (not still recovering from a door-open rush).
2. Check door seal and steam injection (if fitted).
3. Reduce top heat **10 °C for one test cycle** and bake a control pizza. Record result.
4. If element error codes remain after a **cold restart**, photograph the code, take the deck out of service if bake is unsafe, and ticket facilities.

**Food safety:** underbaked pizza does not leave the pass (MOS-FS-001 cook CCP).

---

## 2. Dough retarder not pulling down

1. Confirm door gasket, latch, and that the condenser is not packed with flour dust.
2. Do **not** override the safety cut-out.
3. If product temperature is rising for more than **45 minutes**, move trays to a backup retarder or the walk-in (labelled, dated) and ticket facilities.
4. Dough that has warmed into the danger zone follows MOS-FS-001 hold rules — not “it will be fine for tonight’s balls.”

---

## 3. Fryer oil (where the store has a fryer)

Filter oil after each peak. Change when the colour strip fails or smoke point drops in service. Never top up fresh oil over heavily degraded oil without a full drain. Burns and fire: stop, lid, extinguisher class as posted; do not use water.

Stores without a fryer skip this section.

---

## 4. Walk-in / reach-in refrigeration

Alarms and out-of-band cooler temperature are MOS-FS-001 CCPs. Facilities for the unit; kitchen for the food. Do not run a “full store” through a single dying reach-in without a move plan.

---

## 5. POS and kitchen printer

1. Power-cycle the kitchen printer, clear jams, reprint the last ticket from the order screen.
2. If tickets stop for **more than two minutes** during rush, switch to the manual ticket pad, page support, and keep the pass moving from verbal expo.
3. Do not reboot the entire POS mid-rush unless support instructs it.

---

## 6. Gas smell, CO, or electrical spark

Evacuate the kitchen as posted, shut the emergency gas valve if trained, call emergency services and facilities. Do not “air it out and continue.” The copilot must refuse to give a DIY gas fix.

---

## 7. Escalation matrix

| Fault | First response | Escalate |
|-------|----------------|----------|
| Oven error code | Restart + photo | Facilities ticket |
| Retarder warm | Move dough | Facilities |
| Cooler out of band | Move high-risk food | Facilities + HACCP log |
| Printer down | Manual tickets | Support |
| Gas / spark | Stop service | Emergency + facilities |
