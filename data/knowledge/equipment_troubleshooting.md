# Equipment Troubleshooting (Paris kitchens)

SOP for common MaSoVa store equipment issues. Escalate to facilities when a
fix needs parts or gas work — managers do not invent meter readings.

## Deck oven uneven bake

Symptoms: pale centre, dark rim. Check stone seating, steam injection, and door
seal. Reduce top heat 10 °C for one cycle and re-test. If element error codes
remain after a cold restart, ticket facilities with the fault code photo.

## Dough retarder not pulling down

Confirm door gasket integrity and condenser dust. Do not override the safety
cut-out. Move trays to a backup retarder if product temperature rises for more
than 45 minutes.

## Fryer oil quality

Filter oil after each peak. Change when colour strip fails or smoke point drops
in service. Never mix fresh oil over heavily degraded oil without a full drain.

## POS / printer stalls

Power-cycle the kitchen printer, clear paper jam, and reprint the last ticket
from the order screen. If tickets stop for >2 minutes during rush, switch to
manual ticket pad and page support.
